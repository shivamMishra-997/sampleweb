About

Services

General and restorative treatment for the whole family. Your dentist will advise what suits you after an examination.

  • Check-ups and cleaning

    Routine examination, scaling and polishing to keep teeth and gums healthy.

  • Fillings and restorations

    Repair of cavities and of chipped or worn teeth with tooth-coloured materials.

  • Root canal treatment

    Treatment of an infected or inflamed tooth so that it can be kept.

  • Extractions

    Removal of teeth that cannot be saved, including wisdom teeth where suitable.

  • Crowns and bridges

    Caps for damaged teeth and fixed replacements for missing ones.

  • Dentures

    Removable replacements for missing teeth, fitted and adjusted for comfort.

  • Gum care

    Assessment and treatment of bleeding, swollen or receding gums.

  • Children's dental care

    Check-ups, preventive care and brushing guidance for young patients.

Health tips

Simple habits that look after your teeth and gums between visits.

  • Use a soft-bristled brush and a fluoride toothpaste. Brush last thing at night and once more during the day, covering every surface of every tooth. Spit out the toothpaste but avoid rinsing straight away, so the fluoride stays on your teeth.

  • A toothbrush cannot reach the gaps. Floss or interdental brushes remove plaque from between the teeth, where cavities and gum disease often begin. Ask us which size suits you.

  • How often sugar reaches your teeth matters as much as how much. Frequent snacks and sipped sweet drinks keep teeth under acid attack through the day. Water between meals is the safest choice.

  • Replace it sooner if the bristles splay out, and after an illness. A worn brush cleans less well and can irritate the gums.

  • Bleeding gums, a loose tooth, sensitivity that lingers or a swelling that does not settle should be examined. Many dental problems cause no pain in their early stages.

  • How often you need one depends on your teeth and gums. Your dentist will suggest a schedule that fits you; for many people this is every six months.
