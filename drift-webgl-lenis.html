Most pages treat scrolling as a way to move text up the screen. Here it is an input. Every wheel tick and swipe is smoothed, measured, and handed to a shader that repaints the whole background.

One shader paints the page

A single full-screen fragment shader draws a warped noise field. Scroll depth moves it, your cursor bends it, and scroll speed splits its colour channels apart.

Scrolling with inertia

Lenis eases wheel and touch input toward its target, so motion stays continuous on trackpads, mice, and phones.

Layers that drift

These panels move at different speeds, the band above skews with momentum, and the words light up as you read.

How the scroll becomes light

  1. 1Lenis smooths the input

    Raw wheel and touch events become one eased scroll position.

  2. 2We measure velocity

    Each frame compares scroll positions and smooths the difference into a single speed value.

  3. 3The shader receives it

    Scroll depth, speed, cursor position, and time go in as uniforms. One draw call comes out.

  4. 4The page follows

    Text, bands, and panels read the same signal, so everything moves together.
